> ```
> Another specifiers are not allowed after a struct/unions/enums

Uhmmmm, I think (not sure) they are different things. I think that
commit just tries to avoid things like struct const dummy, but
struct dummy const *s is a different thing and it should be
consumed (and currently ignored ...) by declarator(). I suspect
we are missing a call to declarator() in structdcl() (and maybe
in the unions and enum cases).

> the program. A test[0] in 'tcc' mentioned the following declaration

Indeed, this is clearly a bug in scc.
