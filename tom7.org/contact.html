Tips for e-mailing me

First, I don't want to discourage anybody from sending me mail! I read them all and I enjoy hearing from real people. That said, at times (especially after posting a video) I get overwhelmed and don't respond.

If you want to maximize your chances for a response:

  • I currently make time to write emails at lunch time on Mondays every week. So if you send me an email Sunday night or Monday morning it is most likely going to feel fresh and not get lost.
  • A lot of people ask me for advice, which I guess is flattering, but a question like "do you have any advice about going to grad school?" or even "do you have any life advice?" is both pretty impersonal and takes a lot of mental energy to answer well. If you ask a specific question I'm much more likely to respond.
